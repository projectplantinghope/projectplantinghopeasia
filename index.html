About Our Ministry

Who We Are

Project Planting Hope Asia is a Christian outreach initiative based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. Since 2017, we have been serving communities with the love, compassion, and hope of Jesus Christ.

Our ministry focuses on sharing the Gospel, teaching the Bible, organizing prayer meetings, building relationships, and supporting people in need.

Our Mission

Our mission is to share the love of Jesus Christ with everyone, regardless of their background, and to bring hope and encouragement to communities.

What We Do

Gospel Sharing

Sharing the message of Jesus Christ.

Bible Teaching

Helping people learn God's Word.

Prayer Meetings

Prayer, encouragement and fellowship.

Community Outreach

Serving communities with love and compassion.

Our Vision

We dream of seeing lives transformed through the love of Jesus Christ and stronger communities built through faith and compassion.

Our Heart

We believe every person deserves love, dignity, and compassion. We want to serve with humility and a genuine heart for people.
